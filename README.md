# # Retail Sales & Inventory Intelligence System | Power BI

## Project Overview
An end-to-end retail analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, inventory levels, customer purchasing behavior, product profitability, staff performance, and order fulfillment across multiple retail stores.

The interactive Power BI dashboard transforms retail data into actionable business insights to support revenue growth, inventory optimization, and operational efficiency.

## Key Business Insights
- **Gross Revenue:** $8.57 million.
- **Net Revenue:** $7.93 million after $645K in discounts.
- **Total Orders:** 1,615 orders analyzed.
- **Top Brand:** Trek generated approximately $5.15 million in net revenue.
- **Store Performance:** Baldwin Bikes contributed 72.38% of total sales.
- **Top Category:** Mountain Bikes generated approximately $2.7 million in revenue.
- **Customer Retention:** Approximately 81% of customers placed only one order.
- **Order Fulfillment:** 466 orders experienced delays, representing approximately 28.8% of total orders.
- **Inventory Management:** 36 product lines had critically low stock levels below 10 units.
- **Seasonality:** April recorded the highest monthly order volume, highlighting an opportunity for seasonal inventory planning.

## Dashboard Features
- **Sales Performance:** Revenue, orders, units sold, average order value, and sales trends.
- **Product & Brand Analysis:** Identify top-performing brands, categories, and products.
- **Customer Analytics:** Analyze customer concentration, order frequency, and regional distribution.
- **Staff Performance:** Compare sales and order volumes across employees.
- **Fulfillment & Logistics:** Track delayed shipments, fulfillment rates, and shipping times.
- **Inventory Intelligence:** Monitor stock levels by store and category and identify low-stock products.
- **Discount Analysis:** Evaluate discount amounts and their impact on net revenue.

## Business Recommendations
- Optimize inventory allocation based on product demand and store performance.
- Establish reorder thresholds for high-demand, low-stock products.
- Reduce delivery delays through shipment monitoring and improved carrier coordination.
- Introduce loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Investigate regional sales differences before reallocating inventory or marketing budgets.
- Prepare inventory and promotional campaigns ahead of seasonal demand peaks.
